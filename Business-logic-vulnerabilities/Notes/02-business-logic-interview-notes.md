# Business Logic Interview Notes

## What Is A Business Logic Vulnerability?

Unexpected behavior caused by broken assumptions.

---

## Why Are Logic Flaws Hard To Find?

Because:

```text
They Depend On Context
Cannot Be Easily Automated
```

---

## Common Examples

```text
Price Manipulation
Workflow Bypass
Coupon Abuse
2FA Bypass
```

---

## Root Cause

Bad assumptions.

---

## Best Defense

```text
Server-Side Validation
State Validation
Documented Workflows
```
