# 04 - Impact of Authentication Vulnerabilities

## Overview

Authentication vulnerabilities can have severe consequences because authentication systems protect critical application functionality and sensitive user data.

If attackers successfully bypass authentication controls, they gain access to all functionality available to the compromised account.

---

## Potential Impacts

### Unauthorized Account Access

Attackers may:

- Access sensitive information
- Modify account settings
- Impersonate legitimate users
- Perform malicious activities using trusted accounts

---

### Privilege Escalation

If attackers compromise high-privileged accounts such as administrators, they may:

- Control the entire application
- Access internal systems
- Create backdoor accounts
- Disable security controls

---

### Increased Attack Surface

Even low-privileged accounts may expose:

- Internal pages
- Hidden functionality
- Administrative endpoints
- Sensitive APIs

---

## Real-World Consequences

Authentication vulnerabilities can lead to:

- Data breaches
- Financial loss
- Reputation damage
- Infrastructure compromise
- Regulatory penalties

---

## Key Takeaways

- Authentication vulnerabilities are extremely high impact.
- Compromised accounts can expose sensitive business functionality.
- High-privileged account compromise may lead to full application takeover.

> [!WARNING]
> Weak authentication mechanisms can completely compromise application security.
